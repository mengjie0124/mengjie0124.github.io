Mengjie Zhao @ CNR-ISMAR

I am a PhD researcher in ocean colour remote sensing and oceanography. My research focuses on using satellite observations, in-situ measurements, and data-driven methods to study optically complex coastal waters. I am particularly interested in coloured dissolved organic matter (CDOM), dissolved organic carbon (DOC), and their connections with river inputs and coastal carbon cycling.

My work involves developing and validating ocean-colour retrieval algorithms using multiple independent in-situ datasets, including hyperspectral observations from projects such as HyperBOOST. I compare algorithm performance across datasets, optical water types, and geographic regions, and apply suitable methods to satellite observations of European coastal seas. I am currently investigating how river discharge and flood events influence the distribution of dissolved and particulate materials in coastal waters. By combining remote sensing, field observations, and environmental datasets, I aim to improve coastal water monitoring and better understand how land–ocean interactions affect marine ecosystems and the carbon cycle.

Research Focus

◉

CDOM & DOC Algorithm Assessment

Assessing empirical, semi-analytical, and data-driven retrieval methods using independent in-situ datasets. Comparing performance across optical water types and geographic regions through match-up analysis, uncertainty assessment, and cross-sensor evaluation.

◉

European Coastal Bio-optics

Combining satellite observations, field radiometry, and water-sample measurements to investigate CDOM, DOC, and inherent optical properties in European coastal seas, and evaluate the regional applicability of ocean-colour algorithms.

◉

Floods, River Plumes & Carbon Cycling

Investigating how river discharge and flood events influence coastal CDOM and DOC distributions. Integrating optical, hydrological, and environmental time series to examine river-plume dynamics and their role in land–ocean carbon exchange.
