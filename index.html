Hi, I’m Mengjie.

Ocean colour

I am a PhD student at CNR-ISMAR and Sapienza University of Rome. I study optically complex coastal waters by combining satellite observations, in-situ measurements, and data-driven methods.

My research focuses on retrieving coloured dissolved organic matter and dissolved organic carbon, validating ocean-colour algorithms, and investigating carbon fluxes in dynamic environments shaped by river plumes and flood events.

Research Focus

◉

Ocean Colour

Using multi-sensor satellite observations to investigate spatial and temporal variability in coastal and inland waters.

◌

Coastal Bio-optics

Linking inherent and apparent optical properties to water constituents across optically complex environments.

⌁

River Plumes & Floods

Tracking river-borne material and rapid optical changes following extreme discharge and flood events.

Carbon Fluxes
